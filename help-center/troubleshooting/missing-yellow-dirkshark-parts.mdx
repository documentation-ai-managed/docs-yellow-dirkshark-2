---
title: Missing Yellow DirkShark Parts
description: What to do if you can't find all pieces for Yellow DirkShark.
---

## Symptoms

- Can't locate all required yellow pieces
- Building instructions halted due to missing part numbers

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Your Collection">
    Use the Rebrickable Parts Inventory tool to cross-check which pieces you already have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Alternatives">
    If a part is missing, view suggested alternative pieces on the MOC page or select different colors if allowed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order Missing Pieces">
    Click the "Buy Parts" button to source missing components from partner stores.
  </Step>
</Steps>
