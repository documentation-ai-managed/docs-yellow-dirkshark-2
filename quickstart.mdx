## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have the following:

<Callout kind="info">

- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- An email address for account verification
- Optional: A list of your LEGO pieces for inventory setup

</Callout>

Yellow DirkShark makes it easy to discover and build LEGO projects using your collection. Follow these steps to get started in under 5 minutes.

## Create and Verify Your Account

Sign up quickly to access the library of user-generated designs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Dashboard" icon="monitor">
    Navigate to `https://dashboard.example.com` and click **Sign Up**.

    Enter your email, create a password, and choose a username.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email" icon="mail">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Yellow DirkShark.

    Click the verification link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In" icon="log-in">
    Return to the dashboard and log in with your credentials.

    You'll land on the main library page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
  Save your credentials securely. Use a password manager for best security.
</Callout>

## Browse the Library

Explore thousands of custom LEGO designs shared by the community. Filter by theme, difficulty, or piece count.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Web Browser" icon="globe">
    On the dashboard:

    1. Use the search bar to find designs like "Classic Spaceship".
    2. Browse categories such as `Star Wars` or `City`.
    3. Click a design to view instructions and piece lists.

    Designs show compatibility with your inventory (once added).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Mobile App" icon="smartphone">
    Download the Yellow DirkShark app from your app store.

    1. Log in and tap **Library**.
    2. Swipe through featured projects.
    3. Tap **Build Now** to check against your collection.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Build Your First Model

Build a simple model like a "Mini Castle" using your inventory.

First, add your collection:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Inventory" icon="package">
    Go to **My Collection** > **Add Pieces**.

    Search and add items like:
    
    | Part Name          | Quantity |
    |--------------------|----------|
    | 2x4 Brick Red      | 10       |
    | 1x1 Plate Black    | 20       |
    | Slope 45° Yellow   | 5        |

    Or upload a CSV file with your pieces.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a Design" icon="search">
    Return to the library and search for "Mini Castle".

    Click **Build** to see which pieces you have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow Instructions" icon="book-open">
    Step-by-step images guide assembly.

    Mark pieces as used to track progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For advanced users, query designs via API:

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,Python">
  ```javascript
  const response = await fetch('https://api.example.com/v1/designs?query=castle&limit=10', {
    headers: { Authorization: `Bearer YOUR_API_KEY` }
  });
  const designs = await response.json();
  console.log(designs);
  ```
  ```python
  import requests
  response = requests.get(
      'https://api.example.com/v1/designs',
      params={'query': 'castle', 'limit': 10},
      headers={'Authorization': 'Bearer YOUR_API_KEY'}
  )
  designs = response.json()
  print(designs)
  ```
</CodeGroup>

<ParamField path="query" param-type="string" required="true">
  Search term for designs, e.g., "castle".
</ParamField>

<ParamField header="Authorization" param-type="string" required="true">
  Bearer token obtained after login.
</ParamField>
